About Me

My name is Sarah Byekwaso, a full stack web developer with a unique blend of business, design, and technical skills. I began my journey in Uganda, where I developed a strong foundation in business and customer experience as a Client Relations Manager. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Business Administration with a major in Accounting, and I have always had a creative side — spending time after work learning Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop to explore graphic design.

After relocating to Canada, I took the bold step to transition into the tech industry. I joined a Women in Technology bootcamp, where I trained as a full stack web developer. There, I gained hands-on experience with modern tools. I have since designed and built responsive web applications, focusing on accessibility, clean code, and user-friendly design.

My background in business and client engagement gives me an edge in understanding user needs, communicating effectively with teams, and delivering meaningful digital solutions. I am passionate about using technology to solve real-world problems, especially in ways that support and empower communities.

I am currently building my personal portfolio, exploring freelance opportunities, and continuing to grow as a developer. Whether through design, code, or client collaboration, I bring purpose, passion, and people skills to every project I work on.
